# M2 — oxygen, power, the wrist computer and saving

**Status: implemented; builds on Unreal 5.7; your playtest pending.**
The engine-free oxygen, power and save-file logic has 34 new core tests
(59 in total, all passing with warnings treated as errors).

The tank is now a timer. Your air runs down, the head lamp drains its cell,
and you manage both with the battery pack, spare tanks and the air pocket
under the ceiling near the start.

## Get the build

Close Unreal, run `Tools/Build.bat`, then `Tools/Prepare.bat`, then open
`Paleotrench.uproject` and press **Play**. `Prepare.bat` replaces the old
M1 preparation script. It imports all four tuning tables, creates the water-surface
material and adds the air pocket and two spare tanks to the test tank.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard/mouse | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Head lamp on/off (keeps the level) | F | Y |
| Brighter / dimmer (Off, Low, Medium, High) | Mouse wheel | D-pad up / down |
| Burst strobe (1.5 s, heavy drain) | X | B |
| Wrist computer: tap to toggle, hold to glance | Tab | X |
| Charge the lamp from the battery pack (hold) | C | A |
| Hand-crank the battery pack (hold) | V | Menu |
| Swap to a spare tank (hold 4 s) | T | View |

Swimming, looking, lean, sprint and level-view are unchanged from
[M1](M1.md). The gamepad layout is provisional until the M13 controls pass.

The wrist only comes up when you raise it. Hands tasks (charge, crank, swap)
are one at a time and slow your swimming to 55 %; raise the wrist to watch
their progress. You cannot sprint while busy.

## Reading the gear

**Wrist computer** (lower left when raised):

- Big number: tank pressure in bar, with a bar underneath. Cyan, then amber
  at 50 bar (reserve), then blinking red at 20 bar (critical).
- Depth in metres, measured from the air pocket's water line. It matches the
  numbers on the east wall.
- `AIR n MIN`: time left at your recent breathing rate. It drops when you
  sprint.
- `SPARE n/2`, `LAMP level %`, `PACK %`, compass heading, and dive time since
  you last surfaced.
- The bottom line shows one status: what your hands are doing, a recent event
  (`CHECKPOINT SAVED`, `TANK SWAPPED`, `SPARE TANK +1`, `PACK EMPTY`...), or
  `RESERVE`, `CRITICAL`, `NO AIR` or `BREATHING AIR`.

**Mask LEDs** (left edge of the view, no need to raise the wrist):

- Upper (air): dim green when fine, solid green when breathing air. It slow-blinks
  amber at reserve, fast-blinks red at critical and flashes red when empty.
- Lower (power): off when fine, cyan while charging or cranking, blinking amber
  when the lamp cell or pack is low, blinking red when both are empty.

**Low air:** below 20 bar the view tunnels in, colour drains, a pulse beats in
the vignette, breathing quickens and you swim slower. At 0 bar you can hold
your breath for 6 seconds: swap a tank or reach air, or you black out.

## Saving

- The **air pocket** is the dome in the ceiling above the start. When your
  head breaks the surface, the tank refills (full in about 5 seconds) and the
  game saves a checkpoint (`CHECKPOINT SAVED`).
- **Blacking out** returns you to the last checkpoint, with the gear and
  pickups you had when it saved. Without a checkpoint you restart at the start.
- **Continuing:** pressing Play again resumes from the last checkpoint. To
  start over, open the console (`~`) and type `pt.NewGame`.
- The save file is `Saved/SaveGames/Paleotrench0.sav`.

## Console commands for testing

Open the console with `~` while playing.

| Command | Effect |
|---|---|
| `pt.Oxygen 25` | Set tank pressure (bar) |
| `pt.Lamp 2` | Set the lamp cell (0–20) |
| `pt.Pack 0` | Set the battery pack (0–100) |
| `pt.Spares 2` | Set spare tanks (0–2) |
| `pt.Wrist` | Raise or lower the wrist computer |
| `pt.Save` / `pt.Load` | Save here / reload the last save (testing only) |
| `pt.NewGame` | Delete the save and start over |

## Adjust the numbers

Four CSV files in `Data/` hold every value. Edit them, then close Unreal and
run `Tools/Prepare.bat`, or ask me to reimport them into the open editor.

| File | What it controls |
|---|---|
| `OxygenTuning.csv` | Tank size, breathing rates (bar/min) for rest, swim, sprint and cranking, reserve/critical levels, air-pocket refill rate, swap time, starting and maximum spares, breath-hold time, low-air slowdown. |
| `PowerTuning.csv` | Pack and lamp-cell capacity and starting charge, charge rate and efficiency, crank rate, Nightmare cell leak (0 for now), flicker threshold, burst length. |
| `LampLevels.csv` | Per level: drain per second, brightness (lumens), reach (cm) and beam angles. |
| `SwimTuning.csv` | As in M1, plus `BusySpeedScale`: swim speed while the hands are busy. |

Default timings: a full tank lasts 20 minutes resting, 12.5 swimming and about
7 sprinting. A full lamp cell lasts about 17 minutes on Low, 7 on Medium and
under 3 on High. Refilling an empty cell takes 5 seconds and a quarter of the
pack; cranking that quarter back takes 50 seconds.
